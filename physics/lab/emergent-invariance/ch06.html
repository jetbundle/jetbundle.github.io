chapter 6 — statistical mechanics: the synthesis

Liouville, ensembles, $Z$, and fluctuations in one continuous narrative

6.1 phase space and Liouville

for $s$ degrees of freedom, phase space is $2s$-dimensional $(q,p)$. a distribution $\rho(q,p,t)$ evolves with hamiltonian flow. Liouville’s theorem: the flow preserves phase-space volume; equivalently, the convective derivative of $\rho$ along trajectories vanishes for equilibrium distributions that depend on constants of motion such as $H(q,p)$.

6.2 microcanonical ensemble

isolated system at fixed energy: all accessible microstates on the energy shell (or in a thin shell) carry equal probability. entropy $$S=k_{\mathrm{B}}\ln \Delta\Gamma(E)$$ with $\Delta\Gamma$ counting states or measuring shell volume depending on the classical vs quantum modeling choice.

6.3 canonical ensemble

system weakly coupled to a heat bath at temperature $T$. maximizing entropy subject to fixed average energy yields gibbs weights $$p_n=\frac{1}{Z}e^{-\beta E_n},\qquad Z=\sum_n e^{-\beta E_n},\qquad \beta=\frac{1}{k_{\mathrm{B}}T}.$$ helmholtz free energy $F=-k_{\mathrm{B}}T\ln Z$ generates $U=\langle E\rangle$ via $U=-\partial_\beta\ln Z$, and all standard thermodynamic potentials follow by Legendre transforms.

6.4 classical partition integral

in the semiclassical counting convention, $$Z=\frac{1}{(2\pi\hbar)^s}\int e^{-\beta H(q,p)}\,d^sq\,d^sp.$$ separable $H=K(p)+U(q)$ factorizes $Z=Z_K Z_U$.

6.5 grand canonical sketch

exchange particles with a reservoir at chemical potential $\mu$: $$\Xi=\sum_N e^{\beta\mu N}Z_N.$$ average particle number follows from $\partial_\mu\ln\Xi$.

6.6 fluctuations and response

near equilibrium, entropy expansions are quadratic (ch.3–4 link): small macroscopic deviations are gaussian. energy fluctuations satisfy $$\langle (\Delta E)^2\rangle = k_{\mathrm{B}}T^2 C_V$$ in the canonical ensemble, with $C_V=\partial U/\partial T$.

6.7 thermodynamic limit and equivalence

as $N,V\to\infty$ with $N/V$ fixed, relative fluctuations $\sim N^{-1/2}$ collapse ensemble differences between microcanonical and canonical descriptions of the same intensive thermodynamics.

6.8 worked problems (condensed)

ideal gas (classical, identical particles). single-particle partition function in volume $V$: $$z_1=\frac{V}{\lambda_{\mathrm{th}}^3},\qquad \lambda_{\mathrm{th}}=\sqrt{\frac{2\pi\hbar^2}{mk_{\mathrm{B}}T}}.$$ for $N$ indistinguishable molecules, $Z=z_1^N/N!$. stirling on $\ln N!$ recovers extensive entropy — the sackur–tetrode constant in three dimensions.

energy variance shortcut. $\partial_\beta^2\ln Z = \langle E^2\rangle-\langle E\rangle^2$.
