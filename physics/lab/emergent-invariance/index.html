this cluster is a draft curriculum spine: discrete symbolic counting, continuous aggregation (Riemann → phase integrals), linear spectral structure, high-dimensional probability, local-to-global topology, then statistical mechanics as one partition-function story. pages here link within physics/lab, to the jetbundle-yt deck runner, and to symplect-canvas; the public physics index does not list this area yet.

symplect parallel (repo symplect/). the same design move appears there in code: a mathematical IR encodes state and geometry, structure-preserving integrators enforce invariants stepwise, and multiple renderers read one truth. this lab is the expository analogue for equilibrium: encode microstates → compress sums to integrals → diagonalize energy forms → read typical sets and $Z$.

convention: inline and display math use the same KaTeX setup as physics/classical/index.html. thermodynamic $\beta$ is $1/(k_{\mathrm{B}}T)$ where needed; the notes sometimes write $\beta=1/T$ in reduced units.
