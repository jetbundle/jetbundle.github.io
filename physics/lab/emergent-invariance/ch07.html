chapter 7 — emergent invariance (synthesis and extensions)

microscopic freedom + constraints $\Rightarrow$ macroscopic laws

7.1 six-stage spine (recap)

  1. ch.1 — symbolic counting, events, $\Omega$, multinomial multiplicities, discrete $Z=\sum e^{-\beta E_i}$.
  2. ch.2 — Riemann/Darboux limits, $Z\approx\int g(E)e^{-\beta E}\,dE$, Jacobians for changes of variables.
  3. ch.3 — vector spaces, spectra, quadratic energy forms, markov fixed points.
  4. ch.4 — high-dimensional volumes, gaussians, CLT/concentration.
  5. ch.5 — topology, groupoids, quotients, defects and symmetry breaking as global shape data.
  6. ch.6 — Liouville, ensembles, classical phase integrals, fluctuations tied to response functions.

the unifying object is always the partition function $Z$ (or $\Xi$), whether written as a sum, an integral, a path integral, or a determinant after gaussian integration.

7.2 philosophical core

once state space is large enough and constraints (energy, symmetries, topology) are enforced, the surviving macroscopic description becomes low-dimensional and stable: this is not an extra axiom bolted onto probability, but the geometry of typical sets and the analyticity of $\ln Z$ in the thermodynamic limit.

7.3 extensions (pointers)

  • quantum / QFT: $Z=\mathrm{Tr}\,e^{-\beta\hat H}$ and euclidean path integrals $Z=\int \mathcal{D}\phi\,e^{-S_E[\phi]}$.
  • information theory: maximum entropy under linear constraints reproduces gibbs weights; sanov / large deviations refine rare events.
  • topological phases: invariants persist under disorder — ch.5 morphisms reappear in protected sectors.
  • symplect (software). a practical “preserve structure while aggregating time” stack lives in the symplect/ tree: declarative models, SAV-type splitting integrators, and reproducible figure pipelines — complementary to this mathematical lab, not identical to it.

7.4 open questions (research taste)

  • rigorous bridges between large deviations and topological sector counts
  • non-equilibrium groupoids: time-dependent morphism sets and entropy production inequalities
  • classification of phase transitions via singularities of $Z$ coupled to cohomological data

7.5 appendix roadmap (for a longer book draft)

D. source maps to standard texts (Rudin/Apostol; Axler/Strang; Durrett; Brown–Higgins; Pathria/L&L).
